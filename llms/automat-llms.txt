# Automat

## About
Automat builds AI agents that operate computers the way people do. We replace legacy RPA tools like UiPath, Automation Anywhere, and Blue Prism with intelligent, self-healing managed automations. Founded in 2022 by ex-Google engineers Lucas Ochoa and Gautam Bose. Backed by Felicis, Khosla Ventures, Initialized Capital, and Y Combinator. $19M raised.

## What We Do
- UI-Based AI Agents (RPA): Browser and desktop automation using Computer Use -- agents that see screens, click buttons, and adapt when UIs change. Works on any software including SAP GUI, Citrix, and legacy web portals without APIs.
- AI Document Extraction (IDP): Vision language models that extract, classify, and process data from unstructured documents -- invoices, claims, mortgage applications, medical records.
- API-Based Automations (iPaaS): Orchestrate API integrations alongside UI and document automation in unified workflows.
- Managed Service: Forward-deployed engineers build, deploy, and maintain automations. Customers share a screen recording or SOP; we deliver production automations in weeks.

## Key Differentiators
- Self-healing agents that adapt when UIs change (legacy RPA breaks)
- Computer Use technology: AI agents that see screens and use mouse/keyboard like humans
- Managed service model: no RPA developers or consultants needed
- 10x faster deployment, 80% lower operational cost vs legacy RPA
- Works on any platform including SAP, Citrix, Windows, and web portals
- SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA, ISO 27001 certified
